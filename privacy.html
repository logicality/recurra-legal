Privacy Policy

Recurra is designed with privacy in mind. This page summarizes how the app handles information.

Accounts and data

Recurra does not require an account. Your task data is stored locally on your device.

How we use your information

We do not sell your personal data. The app does not show ads. Recurra does not track you across other apps or websites for advertising or analytics purposes.

Purchases

Recurra is free to try for 30 days, after which a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited task creation and editing. There is no subscription.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We use RevenueCat to validate and manage purchase status across reinstalls and devices signed into the same Apple ID; their service receives a pseudonymous purchase identifier from Apple, never your name, email, or task contents. Apple’s and RevenueCat’s privacy practices apply to that flow.

Your trial start date and purchase status are also cached locally on your device so the app works offline.

Deletion

You can remove app data from your device by deleting the app, subject to your iPhone settings. See Support for a short guide.
